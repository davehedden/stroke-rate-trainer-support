# Stroke Rate Trainer: Support

Stroke Rate Trainer is a simple metronome for swimmers and coaches. It plays a beep at a tempo you choose, in seconds per stroke cycle (0.20 to 3.00), so athletes can learn the tempo they are trying to hit. It works on iPhone and iPad (iOS 15 or later) and needs no account or internet connection.

## Getting started

In the app, open the **Settings** tab and tap **View Instructions** for a full guide. The basics:

- **Set a tempo:** drag the slider, fine tune with the **-** and **+** buttons (0.01 each), or pick an event on the wheel.
- **Play Beeps:** plays a beep every tempo seconds. You can change the tempo while it plays.
- **x2 / ÷2:** double or halve the tempo to switch between one beep per cycle and one beep per arm.
- **Take Rate:** tap once at the start of a cycle and again after counting 1, 2 or 3 cycles. The app sets the tempo from the time between taps. Choose how many cycles you count in Settings > Cycles to Count.
- **Target Ranges:** shows the fastest to slowest stroke tempo among the top 8 swimmers in each event in the 2024 NCAA data (short course yards).

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I change the events on the wheel?**
Yes. Go to Settings > Edit Events. You can rename events, change their tempos, and add, delete or reorder them. "Restore Default Events" brings back the original list.

**Do the beeps play with the screen locked or the silent switch on?**
Yes. Beeps keep playing with the screen locked, ignore the silent switch, and mix with music from other apps.

**The beeps stopped by themselves.**
Beeps stop on purpose if a phone call or Siri interrupts, or if headphones or a Bluetooth speaker disconnect. Press Play Beeps to start again.

**How do I make the beep easier to hear at the pool?**
In Settings, set the beep pitch to High (the default). It carries best over pool noise. Turning up the volume or using a Bluetooth speaker also helps.

**The Take Rate button is greyed out.**
It is disabled while beeps are playing. Stop the beeps, then tap Take Rate.

**Where does the Target Ranges data come from?**
The 2024 NCAA top 8 stroke rate data, short course yards, courtesy of Russell Mark from ASCA.

**Does the app collect my data?**
No. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy).
